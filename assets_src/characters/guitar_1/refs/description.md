# Guitar 1

## Character
- white male, 40 year old
- muscular build
- short dark brown / black beard and stubble (NO gray, white, or salt-and-pepper hair)
- black tshirt, blue jeans, black tennis shoes
- trucker hat with light gray front/bill and black side/back panels

## Instrument
- kiesel vader headless multiscale guitar
    - headles means guitar should have no headstock
    - multiscale means frets are fanned / slanted
- walnut body, maple neck through (maple neck is visible between pickups)
- brown leather guitar strap

## Poses
- **`IDLE_1`**: slight crouch facing right, eyes open, hands on instrument ready to play.  Guitar body should be seen straight-on and neck almost horizontal (15 deg or so)
- **`IDLE_2`**: identical to IDLE_1 (same build, clothing, face, hat, and guitar position), but with a very subtle idle rhythm bob: a slight knee bend with head dropping only a few pixels (~3-4px) rather than an exaggerated crouch. Feet stay firmly planted on the ground.
- **`ATTACK_1`**: crouched lower in an aggressive power stance, slightly aggressive focused expression on face (determined, slight brow furrow, eyes open, mouth closed, NOT an exaggerated angry face or head about to explode), guitar held slightly out front pushed away from body at angle approaching vertical, 75 deg or so. Right (strumming) arm angle bent at 90 degrees with the right hand positioned below shoulder height (hand must stay below the shoulder, not raised above it), ready to strum. Scale, head size, and build must match IDLE_1 proportionally.
- **`ATTACK_2`**: similar to ATTACK_1 (crouched lower in an aggressive stance, slightly aggressive focused expression on face, determined, eyes open, mouth closed, NOT an exaggerated angry face or head about to explode) but right arm extended straight downward across the guitar body (right hand below guitar body) as if it has just been strummed hard. Scale, head size, and build must match IDLE_1 proportionally.
- **`ULTIMATE`**: standing more upright, guitar held as if it were a rifle.  Guitar body facing camera, neck horizontal.  Character is siting down the upper horn of the body like it's a rifle scope.  Right hand on lower horn like a trigger.
- **`HIT`**: body reeling back similar to IDLE_1, but head/face turned back to the left (whiplash from impact) with an exaggerated pained grimace on his face.
- **`DOWN`**: crouched, head down as if injured, guitar vertical on the ground with left hand on the neck as if using as a support to try to stand.
