# Guitar 2

## Character
- white male, 35 year old
- stocky build but not fat, bald with goatee
- black tshirt, khaki shorts, silver sparkle shoes

## Instrument
- see instrument reference picture
- gray guitar strap

## Poses
- **`IDLE_1`**: see `character` reference file, but make stance slightly more agressive.
- **`IDLE_2`**: basically the same but crouched slightly lower
- **`ATTACK_1`**: crouched low, guitar held near knees with neck near horizontal.  Right (strumming) arm poised above strings about to strum.
- **`ATTACK_2`**: similar to ATTACK_1, same wide crouched stance and head angle. The right arm extends down IN FRONT OF (occluding/overlapping) the green guitar body, with the right hand resting/swept near the bottom edge of the guitar body as a follow-through down-strum. The arm must be visibly in front of the guitar, NOT behind it.
- **`ULTIMATE`**: character faced toward camera, feet shoulder distance, guitar hanging neutral.  Facing up toward sky, hands in air as if summoning a storm.
- **`HIT`**: similar to IDLE_1 but as if the character has just been hit. eyes flinching and body reeling back.
- **`DOWN`**: seated on ground, head bowed, left leg straight, right leg bent with knee near chest.  Guitar vertical on the ground with right hand on the neck.
