# The Worm

## Character
- large worm/serpent, loosely inspired by Dune sandworms
- instead of a head it has a giant mouth lined with teeth with a large psychedelic eye inside
- strictly NO eyelids (this creature has no eyelids; mouth/teeth close around the eye instead)
- eye palette consists of: Black, Blue, and Purple (NO stray magenta in the sprite or eye)
- body length and proportions must stay strictly to scale across ALL poses (in attack poses, the worm is lunging forward from the idle posture, NOT unnaturally stretched or elongated)

## Instrument
- none

## Poses
- **`IDLE_1`**: coiled on the ground, tall S-curve neck held high, facing left like a viper preparing to strike. Tail tip visible behind the coil on the right. Eye features black pupil/outlines, light cyan-blue and purple swirls.
- **`IDLE_2`**: derived from `IDLE_1` with slight movement:
  - Tail tip moves laterally.
  - Head bobs down slightly for an idle breathing cycle.
  - Eye color shift: Replace the light blue part of the eye with a darker, pure blue. Purple parts and black pupil remain identical to `IDLE_1`.
- **`ATTACK_1`**: lunging forward to the left from the idle position. The worm body is slightly narrower to match the exact thickness of idle poses. The tail ends shortly after touching the ground on the right (does NOT cross behind the body or loop around), keeping overall length and scale identical to idle_1. Head thrusting forward to strike, mouth wide open with sharp needle teeth. Normal eye colors (light blue and purple).
- **`ATTACK_2`**: secondary lunge / follow-through snapping forward toward the left with the same narrower body and short grounded tail. Head pulled back slightly toward the tail in recoil. Eye color shift: light blue replaced with pure darker blue (matching `IDLE_2`), purple parts remain identical.
- **`ULTIMATE`**: similar to idle but stares directly at camera, eye open wide.
- **`HIT`**: wincing in pain from impact! Head pitched back / wincing, mouth partially closed around the eye as if wincing, eye facing toward the camera. Cohesive natural anatomy to scale with idle poses.
- **`DOWN`**: coiled in a pile on the ground, head lying on ground/body. No eyelid! Mouth/teeth closed slightly around the eye. A puddle of liquid matching the eye colors swirls on the ground below the eye/mouth like blood.
